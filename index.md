# Data in Motion

Data in Motion is a YouTube channel of animated explainers about data engineering, starting with the Spark in Motion series on how PySpark works under the hood.

Watch the channel: https://www.youtube.com/@DataInMotionYT

## Spark in Motion Uploader

Spark in Motion Uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of the Data in Motion channel. It uploads the channel's own videos, thumbnails and captions to that channel and adds them to the channel's playlists, using the YouTube Data API. It is not offered to other users and does not access anyone else's account.
